VICE-FN release notes
=====================

1.1 (2026-10-25)
----------------
- Telnet sessions end when the server closes them. Terminal programs such as
  CaTer used to print old data over and over after you logged out.
- Mac: FujiNet-PC now has the fixes the Linux packages already had in 1.0:
  - the clock: setting the time zone from the C64, and every time format;
  - N: paths typed on the C64 in CD, PWD and the file commands (rename,
    delete, mkdir and the rest), and generated GUIDs;
  - NEW DISK makes disk images.
- The release files are named with their version:
  VICE-FN_AppleSilicon_v1.1.zip and .dmg, and
  vice-fujinet_1.1_<release>_<arch>.deb.

1.0 (2026-10-23)
----------------
- First release: VICE-FN_AppleSilicon for Apple silicon Macs, signed and
  notarized, and vice-fujinet packages for Ubuntu 24.04 and Debian 13 on
  amd64 and arm64.
